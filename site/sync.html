Sync quests

One click in DFO Online sends every quest you are on to the guide, with exactly where to go next.

1. Put the button on your bookmarks bar

Drag this button up onto your bookmarks bar (no bar? press Ctrl+Shift+B, or ⌘+Shift+B on a Mac):

⚔ Sync quests

Dragging does it; clicking it here does nothing.

2. Click it while you play

  1. Have DFO Online open on daggerfalljs.dev, logged in.
  2. Click ⚔ Sync quests on your bookmarks bar.
  3. The guide opens (or comes to the front) on My quests, with each quest's next step.

Your online character's save is read from DFO's own account service, on DFO's page: your login never leaves daggerfalljs.dev and nothing is stored on this site's server; the guide keeps your quests in this browser only. DFO saves an online character every few minutes and when you switch tabs, so a sync shows your progress up to then. Playing offline? Save (F9) first.

If your browser blocks the window, allow pop-ups for daggerfalljs.dev.
