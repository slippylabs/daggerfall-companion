Daggerfall Quest Guide

Load your quests straight from your DFO Online save (My quests tab) and see where to go and who pays, look up any town or dungeon with real travel times, read what a quest wants and who pays the reward, and see every building in a town.

    Type (or paste) a quest entry or what the quest giver said, and the guide finds the quest and the towns, dungeons and buildings it names. DFO Online's journal can't be copied, so the easiest way is to load your save in the My quests tab: every quest you are on comes in with its real names.

          The quests you are on, kept in this browser. Load them from your game save (below), and load it again after you play to bring them up to date. Every trip is timed from "I am in" above.

          Built from Daggerfall's own game data (freeware from Bethesda Softworks) with DFO Online's readers and Daggerfall Unity's quest scripts and travel rules (both MIT). Roads, tracks and rivers on the map are Hazelnut's Basic Roads, the same roads DFO Online draws. Thank you, Hazelnut. Travel times are the game's own fast-travel formula.
